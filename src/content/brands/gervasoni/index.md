Founded in 1882 in Udine, Italy, Gervasoni has evolved from a small wicker-weaving workshop into an internationally recognized furniture manufacturer. Led by third-generation brothers Giovanni and Michele Gervasoni, the brand is known for its informal yet utterly modern aesthetic, shaped by the long-standing artistic direction of designer Paola Navone. With its flagship store in Milan, Gervasoni is represented in Boston exclusively by Showroom.
