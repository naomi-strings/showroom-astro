Recently renovated, Showroom has become the hot furniture shop in town. Its cast of suppliers, which includes Cappellini, Promemoria, Driade, [Flexform](/brands/flexform/), and Gervasoni, reads like an Italian-design honor roll. Owner Doug Gates, who created the 3,000-square-foot airy space in the city’s chic Park Plaza District, also offers to solve your storage problems with [Poliform](/brands/poliform/) closets and to help outfit your kitchen with Varenna’s sleek cabinetry.
