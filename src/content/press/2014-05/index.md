Doug Gates, who owns Showroom, makes the pilgrimage each spring [to Salone del Mobile in Milan] to identify trends and source new pieces for his store. His top pick this season is the A.B.C.D. Chair designed by Antonio Citterio, which Gates calls "the perfect recliner in disguise."
