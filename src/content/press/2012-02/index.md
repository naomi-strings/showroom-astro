The steadfast contemporary designers...continue to offer a new point of view. Antonio Citterio's deceivingly comfortable Evergreen Sofa for [Flexform](/brands/flexform/) is comprised of a series of down-wrapped upholstered elements in warm autumn neutrals that float effortlessly on a rigid metal frame covered in leather.
